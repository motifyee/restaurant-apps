Milestones of National Impact

Each milestone represents a promise fulfilled for the people of Egypt.

1957

Founding of Dumyat Public Hospital

Opened as a national public hospital to provide accessible care to all citizens.

1989

Regional Trauma Center Accreditation

Achieved Ministry accreditation for trauma response and emergency surgery.

2008

Cardiac Care Expansion

Launched advanced cardiac catheterization and ICU monitoring units.

2016

National Research Recognition

Recognized for clinical research improving maternal and neonatal outcomes.

2023

Digital Health Transformation

Implemented unified electronic records and tele-consultation triage.
